New Computer Modern Sans typeface by Antonis Tsolomitis (Samos, Greece)
Source: https://ctan.org/pkg/newcomputermodern

A modern extension of Donald Knuth's original Computer Modern (TeX),
adding full Latin, Greek (monotonic and polytonic), and Cyrillic
coverage. Verified directly against the package's own License.txt
(mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/newcomputermodern/License.txt, checked
2026-09-09): "All fonts are distributed under the GUST license except
NewCM10-Regular, all NewCMUncial, and all NewCM*Devanagari" (which use
GPL3+FontException+DistributionException instead) — NewCMSans10 (the
two files used here) falls under the plain GUST Font License (GFL), not
the GPL3 exception list.
